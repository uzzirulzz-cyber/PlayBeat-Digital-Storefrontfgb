For the **PlayBeat Digital storefront**, I’d structure it as a premium, conversion-focused digital marketplace rather than a generic e-commerce homepage.

### Recommended storefront structure

1. **Top Utility Bar**
   - Instant Digital Delivery
   - Secure Payments
   - WhatsApp Support
   - PKR as default currency
   - Order Tracking

2. **Premium Header / Navigation**
   - PlayBeat Digital logo
   - Search bar: “Search subscriptions, gift cards, software…”
   - Sign In
   - Sign Up
   - Wishlist
   - Cart
   - Account

   Main navigation:
   **Home · AI & Productivity · Streaming · Gift Cards · Software · Gaming · IPTV · Smart Projectors · Web Solutions · All Products**

3. **Hero Section**
   Main headline:
   **Everything Digital. One Platform.**

   Supporting text:
   “Premium subscriptions, AI tools, gift cards, software, entertainment and business solutions — delivered through one trusted digital marketplace.”

   CTAs:
   **Shop Now**
   **Explore Categories**

   Use a dark navy/black premium background with silver highlights, subtle blue/orange gradient glow, glassmorphism cards, and floating product visuals.

4. **Shop by Category**
   Large premium tiles for:
   - AI Tools
   - Streaming Accounts
   - Gift Cards
   - Gaming
   - Software
   - VPN & Security
   - IPTV
   - Smart Projectors
   - Business Web Solutions

5. **Trending Now**
   Product cards showing image, title, starting price, rating, discount badge, wishlist, Quick View, Add to Cart, and Buy Now.

   Highlight products such as:
   - ChatGPT
   - Google AI Pro / Gemini
   - Canva Pro
   - CapCut Pro
   - Netflix Premium
   - Spotify
   - YouTube Premium
   - NordVPN
   - Grammarly
   - ElevenLabs

6. **Gift Cards Section**
   Feature:
   **PlayStation · Xbox · Steam · Apple · Google Play · Razer Gold**

   Add filters for region and denomination.

7. **Streaming & Entertainment**
   Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max, YouTube Premium, Spotify and other available services.

8. **Business Solutions**
   This should be visually different from ordinary product cards.

   Headline:
   **Build Your Business With PlayBeat Digital**

   Services:
   - Business Websites
   - E-commerce Stores
   - CRM Systems
   - Admin Dashboards
   - Custom Web Apps
   - Automation
   - Enterprise Solutions

   CTA:
   **Request a Quote**

9. **Smart Projectors**
   Dedicated premium physical-product section with large photography, specifications, stock status, delivery information and product comparison.

10. **Why PlayBeat Digital**
   Use 4–6 trust cards:
   **Secure Checkout · Fast Fulfilment · Verified Products · Customer Support · Competitive Pricing · Business Solutions**

11. **Deals / Best Value**
   Separate horizontal sections:
   **Hot Deals**
   **Best Sellers**
   **Fresh Arrivals**
   **Best Value**

12. **Premium Footer**
   Include company information, Contact, WhatsApp, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund Policy, Shipping/Service Policy, support links, account links, payment-method logos and social-media icons.

### Visual direction

I would use **deep navy + charcoal + black**, large clean typography, silver borders, subtle glass panels, premium shadows, restrained gradients and more whitespace. Avoid making the homepage look like a crowded marketplace. Products should feel curated and expensive even when the selling price is low.

The storefront should also keep **customer authentication completely separate from the admin system**: `/signin`, `/signup`, `/account`, `/cart`, `/checkout` and product/category routes should never expose or redirect users into the admin dashboard.
